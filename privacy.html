Pace Calendar privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Pace is a calm calendar and planner for iPhone, made by Ilse Placencia (Australia). This page explains what Pace stores and why. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your email address, your name if you give it, and a sign-in id. You can sign in with email, Google or Apple. If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we only receive Apple's private relay address.
  • Your plans: the tasks, events, goals, habits, to-dos, notes and settings you add. They are saved to your account so they come back on a new phone.
  • Support messages: if you tap the bug or feedback icon, we receive your message, your account email (unless you choose anonymous feedback), the app version, iOS version and device type.

2. What stays on your phone

Calendar access (if you allow it) is used on your phone to show and add your events. Voice and camera are used only when you press the talk or scan button. Speech is processed on your device whenever your iPhone supports it. Pace does not upload your calendar or camera images to us.

3. Why we use it

Only to run the app (sign in, save and show your plans, send the reminders you set) and to answer your messages. We do not use your plans for advertising or to train AI models.

4. Who handles it

Google Firebase (sign-in and storage of your account and plans), Supabase (support messages) and Resend (emails that confirm we received your message).

5. Delete your account

Open Pace, go to More, Settings, and tap Delete my account. This erases your plans, settings and profile from our servers and removes your sign-in. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also revoke the Apple connection. You can also email hello@pacecalendar.xyz and we will delete it within 30 days. Support messages you sent can be removed on request. Items in goals you shared with other people may stay visible to those people.

6. Children

Pace is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

7. Security and changes

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so one person cannot read another person's plans. No online system is completely risk-free. If this policy changes we will update the date above.

8. Contact

Questions or requests: hello@pacecalendar.xyz, or use the bug or feedback icon inside Pace. We reply within 24 hours.
